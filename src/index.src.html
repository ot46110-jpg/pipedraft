How to draw

  • View: one finger turns the picture, two fingers pinch to zoom and pan. Iso, Top, Front, Right and Fit are at the top.
  • Pipe: choose Pipe, tap the grid to start, point along an axis (Auto picks it), tap to place. Type an exact length in mm (1500, 1,500 or 1200+300) and press the arrow. With an Apple Pencil, aim and lift: once the Pencil is used, fingers only move the view (pipe settings, Input, changes that). Snaps show as a dot: white end or nozzle, blue midpoint, green lined up with the drawing. Orange dots are free ends to carry on from. ⌫ takes back the last pipe, a tap on the white start dot or Finish run ends the line.
  • Parts: pick a part, then tap a pipe for valves, flanges and fittings, or the floor for equipment. Branches, reducers and supports follow the same way.
  • Select: tap anything to edit it. Arrows move equipment (X, Y, Z) and slide valves and parts along their pipe. A pipe can take a new size, schedule and line number for its whole run. Drag the orange ball at either end of a selected pipe to stretch it, or an arrow to slide the whole pipe sideways (pipes along the move stretch with it). Run selects the whole run. The bar next to the selection has size − +, Valve, Multi and Delete. Drag a box with the Pencil on empty space, or tap Multi, to select several parts and move, copy, resize or delete them together. Tap again on parts that sit together to reach the next one.
  • Check list: the number on the BOM button is the count of things to look at. Tap a warning that names a pipe to jump to it.
  • Sheets: the ⋯ menu has the isometric sheet (pinch to zoom, Save PDF in A3 or A4, one PDF for all lines), the P&ID, the line list and the cut list.
  • Your own parts: Parts, Equipment, + New, or ⋯, Project, Custom parts. Draw equipment from boxes, cylinders and spheres.
  • Keep your work: backups are taken as you work, but save a project file from ⋯, File, Save project now and then. Files keep the project name, drawing number and revision.
